Method framing

How to explain your research on the website

A good product site needs a short, credible explanation of the method. This section gives you a clean structure you can later rewrite with your exact study details.

1

Data input

Traffic observations from your output CSV files are aggregated by station and year to create comparable daily profiles.

2

Peak detection

Congested segments marked by your speed-based peak method are split into AM and PM windows using their detected start times.

3

Change metrics

For this pilot, each station-year is summarized with median peak start time, duration, and the share of days with detectable AM and PM peaks.

4

Public interpretation

The website translates those outputs into intuitive station-level stories now, and can expand to regional change comparisons as more years are added.
